# NTT DATA Named Headline Partner of The European Software Testing Awards 2026

Test Associates is proud to announce NTT DATA , a $30+ billion global leader in business and technology services, as the Headline Partner of The European Software Testing Awards (TESTA) 2026 . For over 12 years, TESTA has celebrated innovation, expertise, and impact across Europe’s software testing and quality engineering community. Open to participants across the UK and Europe, this year’s programme spans 18 categories, recognising the companies, teams, and individuals driving the industry forward. The awards will once again be held in London, bringing together some of the brightest minds in software testing for an evening of celebration and networking. As Headline Partner, NTT DATA lends its scale and expertise to an awards programme built on fairness and merit. NTT DATA serves 75% of the Fortune Global 100 and is recognised as one of the world’s leading providers of AI and digital infrastructure, with capabilities spanning enterprise-scale AI, cloud, security, connectivity, data centres, and application services. As a Global Top Employer with experts in more than 70 countries, and as part of NTT Group – which invests over $3 billion annually in R&D – NTT DATA brings a wealth of insight into what quality and innovation look like at global scale. “NTT DATA is proud to be the Headline Sponsor of The European Software Testing Awards. In an era where AI is reshaping industries and accelerating software delivery, quality engineering plays a pivotal role in ensuring innovation is trusted, secure and delivers real business value. TESTA brings together the very best talent in our industry, celebrating excellence and advancing the practices that will define the next generation of AI & Agentic augmented and accelerated software quality. We’re excited to support that mission and spearhead the thought leadership to the ongoing evolution of our profession,” said Shane Hanrahan, Executive Head of AI Quality Engineering, NTT DATA UK & Ireland . “We’re delighted to welcome NTT DATA as Headline Partner of The European Software Testing Awards. Their global reach and commitment to responsible innovation make them the perfect partner for a programme that champions excellence in our industry,” said Grant Farrell, Director, Test Associates . Category Partners TESTA 2026 is also supported by two category partners: Virgosol is a technology-driven quality engineering company helping organisations across Europe and beyond build resilient, compliant and scalable software. Working across banking, insurance, fintech, telecommunications and e-commerce, Virgosol embeds quality as an engineering discipline across the full software development lifecycle – backed by its own R&D-led proprietary platforms. 2i Testing is the UK’s largest pure-play quality assurance and software testing business, with over 600 quality engineers across the UK and a delivery centre in Hyderabad. A preferred supplier to the UK and Scottish Governments, 2i specialises in major technology transformations, ERP implementations, multi-vendor programme assurance, and AI assurance. Event Partner Aspire Systems , a global technology services firm partnering with Fortune 500 enterprises, joins as Event Partner. Aspire’s Quality Engineering practice combines AI-led testing and automation – including its proprietary AFTA (Accelerated Framework for Test Automation) and QE Studio solutions – to help clients across BFSI, retail, healthcare and technology ship reliable software faster. Judging Panel TESTA guarantees impartial, fair judging through an anonymous evaluation process, with all entries assessed solely on merit by experienced senior industry professionals. The judging panel is chaired by Isaac Álvarez Diz , Associate Director of Quality Assurance Engineering at Novartis and a postgraduate lecturer in Software Quality Assurance. With more than 10 years of experience in QA management and delivery, Isaac has helped organisations modernise their QA practices through shift-left testing, test automation, and Agile, SAFe and DevOps methods.
