# Entries Now Open for the Asia Pacific Software Testing Awards 2026

### Key takeaways

- Entries for the Asia Pacific Software Testing Awards 2026 are now open, closing 12 August 2026.
- The awards feature 16 categories judged impartially by an expert panel in Bangalore, India.
- TAPSTA finalists become eligible to compete at the Global Software Testing Awards 2027.


Recognising Excellence in Software Testing and Quality Assurance Across the Asia Pacific Region and UAE Bangalore, India – May 2026 The Asia Pacific Software Testing Awards (TAPSTA) is thrilled to announce that entries are now officially open.

Celebrating innovation, expertise, and measurable impact across the software testing and quality assurance community, the Awards represent one of the highest honours in the industry for professionals and organisations throughout the Asia Pacific region and the UAE.

Entries will close on 12 August 2026 .

With 16 categories available and multiple entry opportunities, this is the ideal moment for teams and individuals to showcase their achievements and be recognised among the very best in the field.

Figure 1: Snippet from the Software Testing Awards About the Awards Held in Bangalore, India, the Asia Pacific Software Testing Awards provide a powerful platform for brands to demonstrate their capabilities and expertise to a highly engaged audience of industry peers.

The awards ceremony doubles as a premier networking event, uniting the brightest minds in software testing for an unforgettable evening of celebration.

Entries are judged with complete impartiality and transparency by an esteemed panel of industry leaders, ensuring that every submission is assessed fairly and rigorously against strict criteria.

How the Judging Process Works The judging process is designed to be thorough, fair, and transparent: Each category is allocated to the most relevant judge based on their expertise, job function, and experience.

Judges score each entry against strict, pre-set criteria. Entries must achieve a minimum score to progress – ensuring only genuinely strong submissions advance.

The top two highest-scoring entries per category proceed to Judging Day, where the full panel collectively votes to determine the winner.

Companies are not required to physically attend Judging Day, making participation accessible to organisations across the region. The number of permitted entries per category varies depending on category popularity.
